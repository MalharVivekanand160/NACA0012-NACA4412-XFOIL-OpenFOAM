IMPORTANT REPORTING DISTINCTION
===============================

"FORMAL CONVERGENCE"
means that the specified OpenFOAM SIMPLE residualControl criteria
were satisfied.

"STABLE ITERATIVE PLATEAU"
means that formal residualControl convergence was not satisfied,
but the aerodynamic quantities of interest showed a bounded,
stable iterative solution over the selected final iteration window.

The two classifications must not be presented as equivalent.
