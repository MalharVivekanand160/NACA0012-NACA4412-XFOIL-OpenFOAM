NACA0012 V9 NEAR-WALL SENSITIVITY DECISION
===========================================

Production mesh:
    Original V9

Sensitivity mesh:
    V9 with approximately +30% first-layer spacing

Purpose:
    Test sensitivity of aerodynamic coefficients and wall y+ to the
    near-wall radial grading. This is a targeted near-wall sensitivity
    study, not a full grid-convergence study.

DECISION
--------
Retain the original V9 mesh as the production mesh.

Use the +30% first-layer-spacing mesh only as a documented
near-wall sensitivity study.

Reason:
At AoA 0 deg, the drag coefficient changed by less than 1%, while
lift and moment remained close to zero as expected for the symmetric
NACA0012. At AoA 14 deg, both CL and CD changed by less than 1%.
Therefore the predicted aerodynamic coefficients show low sensitivity
to the tested near-wall spacing perturbation.

The +30% mesh produces mean y+ values closer to 0.5, but also raises
the local maximum y+ at AoA14. There is therefore no numerical basis
for replacing the already completed V9 production sweep.

The mesh was NOT selected or tuned based on agreement with XFOIL.

Important:
This study demonstrates near-wall-spacing sensitivity only.
It is not a complete grid-convergence study.
