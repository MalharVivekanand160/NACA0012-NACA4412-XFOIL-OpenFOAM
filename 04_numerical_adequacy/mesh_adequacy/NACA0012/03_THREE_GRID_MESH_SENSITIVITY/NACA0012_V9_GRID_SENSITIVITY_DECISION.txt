FINAL ASSESSMENT
------------------------------------------------

The 165,220-cell V9 production mesh is considered sufficiently
mesh-insensitive for the principal aerodynamic force coefficients CL and CD.

Doubling the cell count from 165,220 to 330,750 changed:
- CD by approximately 0.72 % at AoA = 0 deg
- CL by approximately 1.02 % at AoA = 14 deg
- CD by approximately 1.08 % at AoA = 14 deg

Cm exhibits somewhat greater sensitivity at AoA = 14 deg (~3.4 %).

A formal Grid Convergence Index (GCI) is NOT reported because all quantities
do not demonstrate a consistent asymptotic/monotonic convergence sequence
across the three grids. The study is therefore reported as a systematic
three-grid mesh-sensitivity assessment rather than a formal asymptotic
GCI demonstration.

FINAL DECISION:
Retain the 165,220-cell V9 mesh for the production NACA0012 simulations.
