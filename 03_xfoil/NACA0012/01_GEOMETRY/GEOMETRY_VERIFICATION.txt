NACA0012 SHARP-TE GEOMETRY VERIFICATION
=======================================

OpenFOAM geometry definition:
    t = 0.12

Thickness distribution:
    yt = 5*t*(
         0.2969*sqrt(x)
       - 0.1260*x
       - 0.3516*x^2
       + 0.2843*x^3
       - 0.1036*x^4
    )

The -0.1036 coefficient produces zero thickness at x/c = 1,
giving a mathematically closed/sharp trailing edge.

XFOIL source geometry:
    NACA0012_SHARP_TE_V9.dat

Number of source coordinates:
    481

Direct comparison of the XFOIL coordinates against the analytical
sharp-TE NACA0012 equation gave:

    Maximum |y - y_expected| = 1.311705230111e-10
    Mean    |y - y_expected| = 2.711306186345e-11

Coordinate range:
    xmin = 0.0
    xmax = 1.0

Trailing-edge coordinates:
    first point = (1.0,  0.0)
    last point  = (1.0, -0.0)

Conclusion:
The XFOIL source geometry and the OpenFOAM production geometry are based
on the same analytical NACA0012 sharp-trailing-edge definition.

XFOIL subsequently repanels this source geometry using PANE. Therefore
the numerical surface-node locations do not need to match the OpenFOAM
C-grid wall-node locations.
